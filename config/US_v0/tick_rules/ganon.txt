# ---- MAJUU_FLAG (174, daMajuu_Flag_c, d_a_majuu_flag.cpp: the flags of Hyrule, stage Hyrule; execute
# f_021B9730). majuu_flag_move (f_021B92DC) is a cloth of 21 points stepped a tick at a time: the wave
# phase mWave (+0x1DF4) += l_HIO.mWaveSpeed, then for each point speed += force (springs to its
# neighbours, the wind on its normal, gravity), speed *= 0.85, pos += speed (PSVECAdd, PSVECScale; the
# points at +0x16F0/+0x17EC by turns, the speeds at +0x1CD8). A half step takes half the force and half
# the move, and 0.85^h of the damping. Two copies of the update: the two fixed points (15, 20), the rest.
021B9364    split@r0    add             majuu flag move: mWave += l_HIO.mWaveSpeed a tick
021B94DC    vec@r4      bl 028e8d88     majuu flag move (fixed points): speed += force
021B94EC    d@f1        bl 028e8e64     majuu flag move (fixed points): speed *= 0.85
021B94FC    vec@r4      bl 028e8d88     majuu flag move (fixed points): pos += speed
021B966C    vec@r4      bl 028e8d88     majuu flag move: speed += force
021B967C    d@f1        bl 028e8e64     majuu flag move: speed *= 0.85
021B968C    vec@r4      bl 028e8d88     majuu flag move: pos += speed

# point is put at the strand's length from the one before, along (where it is) + (its velocity, the

# and its velocity is its move times m3648. A position-based (Verlet) step: at a step h the forces
# move h^2 of themselves (*hh) and the velocity, a move per step, keeps m3648^h of itself.

021BC4E4    whole       sth             mant: the hit countdown (+0x4282) - 1
021BD214    whole       sth             mant: the dying countdown (+0x4284) - 1
021BD328    whole       sth             mant: the push countdown (+0x365A) - 1
021BCB30    *hh@f15     fadds           mant: z + the cape's bias
021BCB38    *hh@f16     fadds           mant: x + the cape's bias
021BCB3C    *hh@f13     fadds           mant: y + m3654 (gravity)
021BCB40    *hh@f22     fadds           mant: z + the wind
021BCB44    *hh@f26     fadds           mant: x + the wind
