# Link at 60 fps, from the owner's bug list (docs/recompiler-design.md D21; session qa). The same rules as
# config/US_v0/tick_rules.txt; Link is process 168 (the decomp's d_a_player_main.cpp and its .inc files;
# WWHD's mProcVar0.m34D0 is Link +0x6916 here, m34EC +0x6934, m35C4 +0x6A1C).
#
# ---- the hurricane spin (B9: "over too quickly, the dizziness too short"). procCutRoll (f_02441DC0)
# spins while its time left (90 ticks) counts down, tested at entry and then decremented, and turns the
# body by 14000 a tick plus the stick's turn; procCutRollEnd (f_02442134) stands dizzy while its own count
# runs out; procCutTurnMove (f_02442470) counts the hurricane spin's charge, 47 ticks, with the 0 tested
# on the register at once. Each ran a step: at 60 the spin lasted 45 ticks (the trial: 89 -> 87 / 88) and
# turned 28000 a tick, the charge took 23.5.
02441FC0    late        sth             procCutRoll: the spin's time left (+0x6916) - 1 (tested at entry)
02441FB8    spliti      addi            procCutRoll: m34EC (+0x6934) -= 14000 a tick (+ the step's turn)
02442164    late        sth             procCutRollEnd: the dizziness' time left (+0x6916) - 1 (tested at entry)
024424C8    keep:r7     addi            procCutTurnMove: the hurricane spin's charge (+0x6916) - 1, its 0 tested at once
#
# ---- stairs (B12: "Link judders walking up stairs"). setStepsOffset (f_023FF47C) looks a tick's move
# ahead (current.pos + speedF along his angle) for a step up, lifts Link onto it at once and lowers his
# model by 0.7 of the step (m35C4, eased back to 0 by cLib_addCalc). A half step moves half as far: lifted
# a tick's move before the step, Link was still short of it on the next frame, the ground check put him
# back down, and the step was taken again: the model dropped another 0.7 of the step (his eye went 250,
# 237.6, 246.4 where 30 has 250, 258.8, 263.8). The look ahead and the slope's allowance are a step's move.
023FF514    *h:f7       lfs             setStepsOffset: speedF, the look ahead for a step (x and z)
023FF57C    *h:f10      lfs             setStepsOffset: speedF, the slope's own rise over the move
#
# ---- texture animations Link advances by hand (B11: "gliding, Link flickers more than he should"). The
# decomp's simpleAnmPlay and its kin add 1 to an animation's frame a call, and setItemModel (f_02403C24,
# called from Link's execute) has them inlined: the Mirror Shield's glint (it starts at random, cM_rnd() <
# 0.02, then runs a frame a call to its end; WWHD has three copies, the frames at Link +0x4814, +0xE0C and
# +0xF1C) and the hookshot's own frame (+0x6A44). On both frames of a tick the glint played at twice the
# speed: a flash on the shield on Link's back, most visible gliding. f_0240B7F4 runs two more frames up
# and down by 1 a call (+0x5618, +0x5708). The random start is tested on both frames of a tick too (other
# processes draw from the stream in between, so the two tests are independent): its 2% a call is 1% a step.
02403E34    *h@f30      fcmpu           setItemModel: the glint's start, cM_rnd() < 0.02 a tick
02404E2C    *h@f30      fcmpu           setItemModel: the second copy's start
024050CC    *h@f30      fcmpu           setItemModel: the third copy's start
02403E60    *h@f29      fadds           setItemModel: the Mirror Shield's glint frame (+0x4814) + 1
02404E58    *h@f29      fadds           setItemModel: the glint's second copy (+0xE0C) + 1
024050F8    *h@f29      fadds           setItemModel: the glint's third copy (+0xF1C) + 1
02404768    *h@f29      fadds           setItemModel: the hookshot's frame (+0x6A44) + 1
0240BA90    *h@f29      fadds           f_0240B7F4: an effect's frame (+0x5618) + 1
0240BB10    *h@f29      fadds           f_0240B7F4: its second frame (+0x5708) + 1
0240BC0C    *h@f29      fsubs           f_0240B7F4: the frame (+0x5618) - 1
0240BC28    *h@f29      fsubs           f_0240B7F4: the second frame (+0x5708) - 1
